- **No more keyframe storms at low bitrate.** The drone's encoder caps the size of a P frame at twice the average frame at the current bitrate. Where the bitrate is low (the lowest rung, the low-power mode while disarmed, the first seconds after boot or a link loss) that cap was smaller than an ordinary P frame. The encoder then gave up on every frame and sent a keyframe instead, the link could not carry them, and the picture ran up to 0.8 s late or stopped: on 2026-11-15 one flight lost the picture for 34 s after a disarm with a good signal. The cap now never goes below 14 kB per megapixel of the picture (about 20 kB at 1728x816, 68 kB at 2944x1656), which is above ordinary P frames in every camera mode; at the top rungs it works as before. New drone key `venc.superframe_p_floor_kb` (0 turns the floor off); a drone that keeps its own `mabur.toml` on update gets the default.

GS and drone stay compatible with v0.16.1, v0.16, v0.15 and v0.14 (RC 18, same parameter table), and a v0.16.2 ground station updates the drone by itself.

The fix has host tests (mabur host suite, with a test that fails without it) and was checked against the flight logs of 2026-11-15; it has not been run on a drone yet and is not flown.

v0.16.1 notes: calibration no longer runs at pit power.
